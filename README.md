# PoE 1 atlas tree 3.30 planner (temporary)

A quick single-file planner for the Path of Exile 3.30 atlas passive tree, made until the established planners update.

Open it here: https://felix-riermaier.github.io/poe-atlas-3.30/

- Click a node to allocate the shortest path to it. Click an allocated node to remove it and everything that depends on it. Hovering shows a preview of either.
- Mortal and Cryptic Gateways link to their partner in the opposite corner.
- Click a cluster center, or a mechanic in the left list, to highlight every cluster of that mechanic.
- The right panel lists the allocated modifiers, grouped by mechanic, with stacking values summed.
- The build is saved in your browser.

Tree data comes from [poedb.tw](https://poedb.tw/us/atlas-skill-tree/). Path of Exile is a trademark of Grinding Gear Games. This is an unofficial fan tool and not affiliated with either.
